Kelly desk asset drop folder
==============================

Put new desk PNGs, SVGs, or exports here when you are not sure of the final folder.

After copying files, list them below (one line per file):

  filename.png — what it is / which desk piece

Example:
  new-priorities-tab.png — folder tab from Figma export 2026-09-15

Then ask Cursor:
  "Incorporate the desk assets in uploads/desk-incoming"

An agent will move files into public/assets/desk/, public/assets/desk/slices/,
public/assets/desk/figma/, public/assets/arc/icarus/, or public/assets/arctable/
as appropriate. Do not put images in src/.
